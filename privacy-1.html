Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how Exact Crop ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is developed and operated by Azubuike Christian Obiora (Mythbborrn Press) ("the Developer"), based in Delta State, Nigeria. Contact: obioraazubuike78@gmail.com.

Your images never leave your device

Exact Crop processes every image locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Nothing you upload, crop, or export is sent to a server, stored remotely, or seen by anyone else, including the developer. If you disconnect your device from the internet after the page has loaded, the tool continues to work exactly the same, because there is no server-side component involved in processing.

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your photos.
  • We do not require an account, sign-up, or login, and we do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.
  • The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, or microphone. It only reads the image files you choose to open.

Local storage & offline support

Exact Crop is a Progressive Web App. If you install it or revisit it, your browser may cache the app's own files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons) on your device so it loads faster and works offline. This cached data is the app itself, not your photos, and stays on your device.

Third-party services and cross-border transfer

The App's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and the PDF export feature loads a small JavaScript library (jsPDF) from a public content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). As with any website, these providers may process technical data such as your IP address when your device requests a file, under their own privacy policies, and this takes place outside the Chinese mainland. These requests only fetch the app and library code; your images are never sent anywhere. Standard browser network tools (like the DevTools Network tab) can be used to verify this. By using the App, and by accepting this policy when prompted, you consent to this.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 14 in the Chinese mainland). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Your rights and choices

You may contact us at any time to ask whether we hold any information about you, and to access, correct, or delete it. Since we do not collect personal information, there is normally nothing to provide, but we will reply to every request within 15 days. You can withdraw your consent by stopping use of the App and clearing its cached data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will be revised, and we will ask for your consent again if the changes are significant.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to obioraazubuike78@gmail.com.

关于本政策

本政策说明 Exact Crop（以下简称“本应用”、“我们”）如何处理信息。本应用由位于尼日利亚三角洲州的 Azubuike Christian Obiora (Mythbborrn Press)（以下简称“开发者”）开发并运营。联系方式：obioraazubuike78@gmail.com。

您的图片不会离开您的设备

Exact Crop 使用 Canvas API 在您的浏览器本地处理每一张图片。您上传、裁剪或导出的任何内容都不会发送到服务器、不会被远程存储，也不会被任何人看到，包括开发者本人。页面加载完成后，即使您断开网络，工具也能照常使用，因为处理过程不涉及任何服务器端组件。

我们不收集的信息

  • 我们不会收集、存储或传输您的照片。
  • 本应用无需注册账号或登录，我们不会收集您的姓名、邮箱或手机号码。
  • 我们不使用追踪类 Cookie 或第三方广告追踪器。
  • 本应用不会申请访问您的相机、通讯录、位置或麦克风，只会读取您主动选择打开的图片文件。

本地存储与离线支持

Exact Crop 是一款渐进式网页应用（PWA）。如果您安装或再次访问本应用，浏览器可能会在您的设备上缓存应用自身的文件（HTML、CSS、JavaScript、图标），以便加载更快并支持离线使用。缓存的数据是应用本身，而不是您的照片，并且只保存在您的设备上。

第三方服务与跨境传输

本应用的页面托管在 GitHub Pages（GitHub, Inc.），PDF 导出功能会从公共内容分发网络（cdnjs.cloudflare.com）加载一个小型 JavaScript 库（jsPDF）。与任何网站一样，当您的设备请求文件时，这些服务提供方可能会按各自的隐私政策处理 IP 地址等技术数据，该处理发生在中国大陆以外。这些请求仅获取应用和库的代码，您的图片绝不会被发送到任何地方。您可以使用浏览器的网络工具（如 DevTools 的 Network 面板）自行验证。您使用本应用，并在提示时同意本政策，即表示您同意上述安排。

未成年人隐私

本应用是面向一般用户的工具，不面向 13 周岁以下儿童（在中国大陆为 14 周岁以下）。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。如果您认为有儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们将及时处理。

您的权利与选择

您可以随时联系我们，确认我们是否持有您的信息，并要求查阅、更正或删除。由于我们不收集个人信息，通常没有可提供的内容，但我们会在 15 天内回复每一项请求。您可以通过停止使用本应用并清除其缓存数据或卸载来撤回同意。

政策更新

如本政策发生变化，上方的“最后更新日期”将随之修改；若变更较为重大，我们会再次征求您的同意。
